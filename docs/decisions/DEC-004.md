# DEC-004 — Soft delete scope

**Status:** APPROVED  
**Date:** 24/09/2026  
**Owner:** Tech Lead

## Decision

Add `IsDeleted` and soft-delete query filters to the aggregate roots `User`, `Client`, `Product` and `Order`. `OrderItem` does not receive an independent delete flag; it is soft-deleted through its parent Order. Normal queries exclude deleted roots. Management queries may include deleted records only when an explicit management endpoint/filter is approved.

## Rationale

The product approves soft delete but the specification only names `IsDeleted` on User. Applying it to roots preserves history while keeping the normal business views clean.

## Impact

Applies to TASK-006 through TASK-011, TASK-025, TASK-028, TASK-032, TASK-037, TASK-042 and relevant tests.

## Evidence

The DELETE routes exist for Users, Clients and Products; Orders have no public DELETE route but their history must remain consistent.
