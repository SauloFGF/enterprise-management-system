# DEC-017 — Dashboard metrics

**Status:** APPROVED  
**Date:** 24/09/2026  
**Owner:** Tech Lead

## Decision

The dashboard is all-time because the specified Order entity has no date field. Expose these cards:

1. Total active Clients.
2. Total active Products.
3. Total active Orders.
4. Orders Pendente.
5. Orders Concluído.
6. Orders Cancelado.
7. Revenue of Concluído Orders in BRL.

Soft-deleted roots are excluded. Cancelled orders do not contribute to revenue. No charts, predictions or additional periods are included in the MVP.

## Rationale

These metrics are useful, testable and can be calculated from the specified fields without adding an unapproved date dimension.

## Impact

Applies to TASK-048 through TASK-051 and Dashboard tests.

## Evidence

The specification confirms dashboard cards/indicators but not metrics or period.
